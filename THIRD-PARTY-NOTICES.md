- **Paintings in `demo-vault/Assets`:** in the public domain. Johan Christian Dahl, *View of Dresden by Moonlight* (1839), and Abraham-Louis-Rodolphe Ducros, *Night Storm at Cefalù* (c. 1800–1805), both via Wikimedia Commons.
