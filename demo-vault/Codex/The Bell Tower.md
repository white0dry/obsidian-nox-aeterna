The tower at the heart of the old town, and the seat of the Watch. [[North Street]] runs from here to the walls. Its bronze bell is said to have been cast from the armour of the first watchkeepers, and bats roost among the bell frames.

## The Toll

The bell strikes every hour. After thirteen strokes the city gates close. On storm nights the thunder drowns the bell, and the Watch signals with iron whistles instead.

## The Spire

Copper lightning chains run from the spire to the ground, and rainwater follows them down into the cistern beneath the tower.
